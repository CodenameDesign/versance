Section 04 — Logos

Logos

The Versance logos: the lock-up, the icon and the TTE lock-up, in black, Versance blue and white.

LogosBlack, white and blue
  • Use the lock-up by default; use the icon only where the wordmark will not fit
  • Black on light surfaces, white on dark (var(--color-ink)) or photographic backgrounds, and Versance blue (--color-primary, #324efe) on white or light surfaces where the brand colour is wanted. No other colours, no gradients, no effects
  • Never stretch, rotate, outline or re-draw a logo. Scale it proportionally, by width, and let the height follow
  • Clear space: keep at least the height of the icon's inner bars (about a quarter of the logo height) free on every side
  • Minimum size: lock-up 120px wide, icon 24px wide
  • Admin sidebar: lock-up, white, 160px wide (32px high on a phone), padding 24px
